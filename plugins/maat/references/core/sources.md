# Sources

Rules change faster than code: between 2025 and 2026 the FTC's Click-to-Cancel rule was struck down, the ADA Title II deadlines moved, and EN 301 549 V4.1.1 came out. This job re-checks the sources the area files cite.

## Steps

1. List every source in `references/` with its `checked` date and its tag ([primary], [secondary], [to confirm]).
2. Re-open first the ones that are [to confirm], then the ones checked more than six months ago.
3. For each, record: still says the same / changed (what) / gone. Prefer the rule-maker's own page (W3C, Planalto, EUR-Lex, Federal Register) over a summary.
4. Propose the edits to the area files as a draft, with the new `checked` date. A changed rule may change verdicts in past reports: say which controls.

When the network blocks a primary source, say so and keep the tag as it was; never upgrade [secondary] to [primary] without reading the primary.
