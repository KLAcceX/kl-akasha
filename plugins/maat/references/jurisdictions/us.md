# United States

Obligations by control, federal and by state. Add a row when a niche goes live or a state is added; change a row when the rule changes, with the new `checked` date.

| Control | Rule | What it requires | Enforcement | Status | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ACS-01 to ACS-07 | ADA Title III (private businesses) | Accessible websites; no technical rule from the DOJ, WCAG 2.x AA used in settlements (*Robles v. Domino's*, 9th Cir. 2019, cert. denied) | Private lawsuits: 3,117 federal website suits in 2025, up 27% (Seyfarth); about 5,000 counting state courts (UsableNet) | In force | 2026-11-04 [secondary] |
| ACS-01 to ACS-07 | ADA Title II rule, 28 CFR 35 subpart H (state and local government) | WCAG 2.1 AA for web and mobile apps; SaaS sold to these bodies inherits it by contract | DOJ | Deadlines moved by interim rule (20/04/2026) to 26/04/2027 (50,000+ people) and 26/04/2028 (smaller entities) | 2026-11-04 [primary, Federal Register 2026-07663] |
| ACS-01 to ACS-07 | Section 508 (federal agencies and their ICT vendors) | WCAG 2.0 AA | Federal procurement | In force since 18/01/2018 | 2026-11-04 [primary] |
| ACS-07 | FTC Act §5 | No deceptive claims, including "makes any site compliant" | FTC; *FTC v. accessiBe*, US$ 1 million (2025) | In force | 2026-11-04 [primary] |
